Research Objectives & Key Findings

In resource-constrained Industrial IoT edge deployments, gateways cannot inspect 80+ network flow features in real time without packet drops. This suite replicates Houkan et al. (IEEE Access, 2024) to verify whether dimensionality reduction can preserve accuracy while slashing computational overhead.

⭐ Optimal Pareto Frontier: MRMR-12

Reducing input dimensions from 79 baseline features down to 12 MRMR features achieves an 84.8% compression ratio and cuts training time by 92.3%, with a negligible accuracy change of only 0.03% (99.90% → 99.86%).
